Entry for [The Moon Finally Answers Back](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1)
on make.fun — a single line of text, 1 to 240 characters, that the Moon says
back to everyone who has ever stared up at it.

## The line (115 characters)

> Four billion years of wishes, howls, and 2 a.m. promises — and not one of you has ever wished me a good night back.

## Why this line

- **Speaks as the Moon, answering all three:** wishes, howls, and 2 a.m.
  promises are named in the first half, so the line is the Moon talking
  back — not a line merely about the Moon.
- **Funny on one read:** four billion years of being wished upon, and the
  punchline is the one wish nobody ever made — a good night back for the
  Moon itself.
- **The Moon's own voice:** "Four billion years" opens wry, old, and a
  little tired of being wished upon; the comeback turn arrives before the
  line ends.
- **Wastes nothing:** 115 characters, no word explained, no joke explained.
- **Original:** written fresh for this bounty, no recycled quote or
  borrowed line.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the line to
`/output/entry.txt`. Run locally with:

```
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
